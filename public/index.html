Model notes

How the decision is made

At 00:00 UTC the model compares washing once with waiting for the entire horizon. Both paths repeat the preceding seven days’ mean hourly clean power and particle deposition. The recommendation turns positive when the value of extra AC energy exceeds the full wash cost.

Net value = ΔAC energy × tariff − wash cost

From airborne dust to lost output

Fine and coarse particles settle at 0.0009 and 0.004 m/s. The HSU model converts accumulated mass into a soiling ratio. Each path applies that ratio to clean DC power, includes system losses and inverter efficiency, and clips AC power at the inverter limit before summing hourly energy. The published mass range extends to 10 g/m².

SR = 1 − 0.3437 erf(0.17 × mass0.8473)

What the amps mean

The reference load is the highest hourly clean DC power in the trailing seven-day mean profile. Both wash states use that load and the same configured operating voltage. The result is an array-equivalent current gain at that operating point. Matched string readings can be checked separately in the decision calculation.

ΔI = 1,000 × Pref,kW × (SRafter − SRbefore) / VDC

Rain, wash history and field use

The dry outlook assumes no effective rain. Moving the rain control resets both paths at the start of the selected day. It tests timing without assigning a weather probability. Historical rain resets mass when the rolling 24-hour total reaches the configured threshold. The Dubai replay applies its dry-policy wash signals. Imported records use only logged washes. All modeled washes assume zero downtime.

Reference data & assumptions

The Dubai reference covers 8,760 hours in 2025 at 24.7655° N, 55.3730° E. Its 10 MWp DC array is a scaled research case. Energy, current and profit are estimates. The reference tariff and wash cost are study assumptions. Field use needs the plant’s clean-power baseline, DC voltage, wash efficacy and contract rates.
